01 / ABOUT ME THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

A LITTLE
ABOUT ME.

01—03

I'm a Computer Science and Education student interested in using technology to create meaningful learning experiences.

My interest in computer science began with video games. What started as an interest in the technology behind the games I enjoyed grew into a passion for understanding how technology works and what people can create with it.

I've always wanted to teach, so combining computer science and education felt like a natural path. Through classroom experiences, I've become especially interested in making computer science accessible and engaging for younger students.

I believe that problem-solving, creativity, and media literacy are increasingly important in a world shaped by artificial intelligence and rapidly changing technology. I want to help students become more than technology users: I want to help them understand, question, and create with it.
